# Launch messaging and campaigns

From the owner, November 26, 2026. The product name is still "Volley" and changes with the rename.

## The product

**Put your membership to work.**

Volley is free membership and volunteer management software for organizations that depend on their people.

Manage your roster, organize committees, recruit volunteers, fill positions and distribute responsibilities across your membership.

Unlimited members. No per-person fees.

**Request free access**

"Membership management" and "volunteer management" stay in the copy, because they are the categories people search for. Free is how Volley gets in the door, not the whole pitch. The job of the launch product: help organizations manage their people and get more of them involved.

## Campaigns

Each audience gets its own message, built on a problem from the [research](../research/membership-language-2026-11.md).

| Audience | Message | The problem behind it |
|---|---|---|
| Athletic associations | Run your association without running everything yourself. | A large membership with a small staff |
| Professional associations | Give more members a role in your organization. | Members who belong but never take part |
| Community groups | Stop relying on the same five people. | The same few people do everything |
| Boards and committees | Make leadership transitions easier. | Turnover takes the know-how with it |
| Volunteer coordinators | Turn your volunteer list into a working team. | A list of people nobody asks |

## What the product says, and what it doesn't

- It never calls a person "inactive". It shows what each person was offered, what they accepted and what they took part in.
- Renewals and dues appear only for organizations that turn them on.
- No all-in-one claims: no file storage, bookkeeping, project management or newsletter design.
