## Description

Volley is free membership and volunteer management for small organizations. Import your roster from a spreadsheet or your old system's export. Track categories, renewals and dues, take applications on a public page, and ask the right members to staff each event or committee. Members answer from their phone.

## Maker's first comment

I've worked with associations and community groups for years. The small ones keep their members in a spreadsheet and their renewals in someone's head, because the software built for this costs more than they can justify.

Volley is my answer. It's free. You bring your list, set your membership categories and renewal dates, and the tool sends renewal reminders, takes applications, and asks members to help with the work you need done. Each organization is approved by me before it can send email, so the tool stays useful and doesn't become a spam cannon.

Dues payments still happen outside Volley for now. You record who paid and link to the payment page you already use.

I'd like to hear from anyone running a small association, club or nonprofit. What do you track today, and what would make you switch?
