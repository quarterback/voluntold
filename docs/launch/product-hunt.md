Product Hunt allows 60 characters for the tagline.

- Alternative: Free software to organize and activate your members (52 characters)

## Description

Put your membership to work. Volley is free membership and volunteer management for organizations that depend on their people. Import your roster, set up your board and committees, take applications, and ask the right people to fill each job, so the work doesn't fall on the same few. Unlimited members, no per-person fees.

## Maker's first comment

I've worked with associations and community groups for years. The small ones keep their members in a spreadsheet and their renewals in someone's head, because the software built for this costs more than they can justify.

Volley is my answer. It's free, with unlimited members. You bring your list, set up your roles and committees, and the tool asks the right people for each job, so the work spreads past the regulars. When a chair steps down, the next one inherits the notes and the open work. Renewals and dues are there if you have them and hidden if you don't.

I approve each organization before it can send email, so the tool stays useful and doesn't become a spam cannon.

I'd like to hear from anyone running a small association, club or nonprofit. What do you track today, and what would make you switch?
