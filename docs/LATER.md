# Later

Ideas that are not in the pilot, one line each. When a pilot organization needs one, it moves into [SPEC.md](SPEC.md) with a decision record. The order is a rough guess, not a plan.

## Helping organizations work out what to ask for

- **Notes into tasks:** paste a list, notes or a document, and a model drafts tasks for the coordinator to edit.
- **Warm, small tasks:** suggest small, easy tasks for people who haven't done anything in a while, to keep them involved.
- **Work for people who fit nothing open:** suggest a task built from a person's skills and the organization's needs. The demo's first offers only propose work that already exists ([0010](decisions/0010-activation-demo.md)).
- **Weekly drafts:** once a week, draft asks for people who have waited longest, ready for the coordinator to approve.

## Sending asks

- **Email:** the tool sends each ask itself, with Yes and No links.
- **Text messages:** after the organization registers a number with the carriers ([0005](decisions/0005-email-first-then-text-then-slack.md)).
- **Slack:** asks as direct messages with Yes and No buttons.
- **Reminders:** a reminder the day before.
- **Urgent asks:** an ask that goes first to a lead and then to everyone nearby who opted in.

## People and requirements

- **Requirements that expire:** training or checks with an end date, and a warning before they run out.
- **Tiers that change who fits:** how often each person wants to be asked (one time, seasonal, regular, core). The demo shows tiers but doesn't use them for matching ([0010](decisions/0010-activation-demo.md)).
- **Standing roles:** roles such as show host, with yearly requirements and renewal.
- **Hours outside the schedule:** a one-line note of extra hours, for organizations that must report them.
- **Several coordinators:** each sees only their own tasks on Today, and the weekly limit still holds across all of them.

## Bringing data in and out

- **Data API:** a way for other tools to send volunteers and tasks in, and read them out.
- **Google Drive and Sheets:** read old sign-up tabs as history, and write a roster tab back.
- **New people arriving:** a shared link where new people ask to join and wait for approval, with a waitlist.

## AT Protocol

- **Log in with Bluesky:** AT Protocol OAuth for sign-in and posting, replacing the app password ([0008](decisions/0008-pilot-build-choices.md)).
- **Volunteers keep their record:** a volunteer claims their history into their own AT Protocol account.
- **Organization statements:** the organization posts "this person completed training" or "this person did this work", with the person's consent.
- **Following tasks:** a feed or page that shows posted tasks from many organizations in one area.
- **Private data on AT Protocol:** look again once AT Protocol private storage ("Spaces") is out of alpha and reviewed ([research](research/atproto-native-2026-10.md)).
